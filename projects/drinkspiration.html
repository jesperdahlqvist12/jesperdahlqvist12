Drinkspiration

2015 — 2016

Branding, UI & UX

Drinkspiration by Absolut is a cocktail app that serves up personalized drink recommendations based on your taste, ingredients, and mood. Explore a wide range of recipes, complete with generated how-to videos, and get inspired anytime with the app available on iOS and Android.

↓

Bookmark your favorite drinks in your own drink collection, whether you made the drink yourself or tried it in a bar.

Over 3 500 drink recipes based on Vodka, Gin, Rum, Brandy, Whisky, Champagne, Tequila, Triple Sec and non-alcoholic options.
