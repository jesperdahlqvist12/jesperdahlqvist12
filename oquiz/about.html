What's it all about?

Want to guess the flag? Know a lot about cats? It's your time to shine! oQuiz provides you, the gamer, a platform to test your knowledge in various categories. Over 1000 questions and over 10 categories is at your disposal. We're in the works of adding a lot of new categories and questions, so make sure to enable automatic updates for oQuiz.

Rules

The faster you answer right, the more points you will earn. The maximum amount for a right answer is 15 points. If you answer wrong however, we'll take 5 points. Not too harsh, huh? :)

Game on!

Multiplayer

Outwit your friends! Challenge strangers! We use Game Center in order to find you new opponents - so make sure to log in to your Game Center account.

Store

Our store enables you to expand your Categories-library. Fancy some new challenges? Buy a new package featuring a lot of new questions - tailored made just for you.

Tired of the ads? You can simply buy yourself out of misery, by tapping on the "Remove ads"-button, located under Settings.
